## Game modes
### Practice PvP (the lobby / Club Spawn)
- Ranked and unranked 1v1 duels: /queue (or the queue item in your hotbar), /ranked, /unranked.
- Duel a friend: /duel <player>. Rematch: /rematch. Spectate a match: /spectate <player>.
- Tiers (ranked): LT5 (lowest) → HT5 → LT4 → HT4 → LT3 → HT3 → LT2 → HT2 → LT1 → HT1 (best). Tiers are per kit. Play ranked matches to get placed.
- Modern kits: Sword, Crystal, Axe, UHC, Pot, NethOP, SMP, Mace, Spear and more.
- 1.8 kits (true 1.8 combat: no hit cooldown, 1.8 knockback, sword blocking by holding right click, rod combos, instant soup): NoDebuff, Debuff, Gapple, Soup, Archer, Combo, BuildUHC, Classic. Open /queue → the **1.8** tab, or go to the 1.8 Legacy Hall with /legacyhall.
- Arcade modes (1.8 tab too): The Bridge, Sumo, Boxing, Spleef, OITC, KOTH, Parkour. /arcade.
- FFA arenas: /ffa. Practice bots: fight a bot from the queue menu (choose a difficulty).
- Parties and 2v2: /party create, /party invite <player>.
- Edit your kit layout: /kiteditor.
- Stats and leaderboards: /stats [player], /leaderboard. Settings: /settings. Ping: /ping.
- Matches start with a capsule: you stand in a glass capsule that splits and drops you into the arena. Elevators take you to your arena; arenas are built when needed, so sometimes you wait a few seconds.
- While fighting, you only see your own arena.
- 13 hand-designed arenas (castles, palace, colosseum, plaza, sakura, ember, gothic, beach lighthouse, jungle temple, volcano, pirate cove, pharaoh tomb...) plus arcade arenas (Sumo Sky Dojo, Boxing Ring, Bridge castles, Frozen Spleef, OITC Courtyard, KOTH Temple).
- Progression: ranked seasons and Hall of Fame, divisions, clans, Weekly Cup, battle pass, missions, ~300 achievements, titles, daily rewards, replays and match analysis.
- Fight extras: fight HUD (combo, reach, CPS), death recap, auto GG, kill sounds, hit sounds (toggle in /settings).

### Survival SMP
- Join with /smp. Everyone plays the SMP as an equal survival player: no admin or cheat commands for anyone (staff and owner included). Rank names stay but give no advantage.
- Player commands: /home, /sethome, /tpa <player>, /rtp, /warp, /enderchest (/ec), /craft, /trash, /trade <player>, /market, /kit, /namecolor.
- /kit: everyone has a Starter kit; better kits come with ranks.
- Land claims protect your base. If something was griefed or lost because of a bug, open a ticket with your coordinates and screenshots.
- The SMP has its own Nether and End worlds. **Current status: the Nether and the End are closed/locked for now; staff will announce in #📢・announcements when they open.**
- SMP seasons exist. **No SMP reset has been announced.** Any reset will be posted in #📢・announcements first.
- Mending comes from librarian villagers or fishing, not from enchanting tables.
- Staff can watch players with /spectate (hidden) to catch cheaters.
