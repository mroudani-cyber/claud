## Q1: What ranks/packages do mcpvp.club (PvP Club) and PvPHQ sell, for how much, and with what perks? Cosmetics stores, battle passes, crates?

### Takeaway
Both servers run small, simple Tebex-style stores: PvP Club sells a permanent VIP ($19.99) plus a monthly VIP+ subscription ($9.99/30 days, requires VIP), with perks centered on cosmetic customizers (armor trims, shield patterns, display names), kit slots, party size and queue priority. PvPHQ sells two 30-day ranks (HQ $10, HQ+ $20) plus a coin currency ($10 per 1,000 coins, bulk bonuses) used for rank duration, gifting and cosmetics. No battle pass or crate packages were found for either server.

### Cited Findings

**mcpvp.club / PvP Club (general)**
- PvP Club calls itself a "modern Minecraft PvP practice server"; Discord describes a duel queue against random players across many gamemodes, plus party modes: team duels, FFAs, "host battles" and custom fights with friends. — [PvP Club site](https://mcpvp.club/); [mcpvp.club Discord](https://discord.com/servers/mcpvp-club-1477487779498692722)
- Free to join, kits are provided (no gear grind); Java 1.21.2+ (listing says supports Java 1.21 – 26.3); premium account required; Bedrock via play.mcpvp.club. Site has /rules, a store at store.mcpvp.club and a tier-test page at mcpvp.club/tiertest. — [mcserverip.com listing](https://mcserverip.com/ip/mcpvp-club); [mcprofiles.me](https://mcprofiles.me/server/mcpvp.club)
- Player count snapshots differ: one tracker showed 2,790 online, another 5,124/15,000; ranked #5 of 514 on one practice list. — [mcprofiles.me](https://mcprofiles.me/server/mcpvp.club); [mcserverip.com practice list](https://mcserverip.com/mode/practice)

**EULA context**
- Official Minecraft Usage Guidelines permit servers to sell cosmetics, except capes or anything visually imitating a player cape; access must be limited to genuine paid copies. — [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)
- Core rule per guides: paying players may not get gameplay advantages over non-payers; cosmetic ranks (prefix, color, title, particles) are fine; crates, currencies and battle passes are described as gray areas; guidelines can change without notice. — [GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/minecraft/monetising-your-server/); [space-node 2026](https://space-node.net/blog/minecraft-eula-monetization-rules-2026); [mc-node.net](https://mc-node.net/blog/en/minecraft-server-monetization-eula-rules/)

### Inferences
- The 2025-2026 practice-server store norm is small and cosmetic/convenience-heavy: 2-5 ranks, priced roughly $7.50-$45 one-time (Minemen) or $10-$20/month (PvPHQ, PvP Club VIP+). Subscriptions are becoming the norm at newer servers (PvPHQ is subscription-only; PvP Club mixes permanent + monthly).
- Commonly sold perks across all three: queue priority/queue bypass, larger parties and party modes, hosting events/tournaments, more kit slots, name/tag/display-name customization, cosmetic armor/trims, lobby fly/elytra, tab priority. These are convenience/social perks that avoid combat advantage (EULA-safe), although queue priority is a mild access advantage.
- Notable modern twist from PvP Club: monetizing 1.20+ vanilla cosmetic systems (armor trim, shield pattern, shulker color customizers) instead of plugin particle effects.
- PvPHQ's coin currency enables gifting and cosmetics and is a step toward a cosmetics shop; no evidence of loot boxes/crates, which fits EULA caution and gambling regulation concerns.
- Avoid copying cape-like cosmetics (wings/back items resembling capes could be contentious under the cape rule).

### Gaps
- Full PvP Club store category list (whether there is a cosmetics category, kill effects, or unbans) could not be fetched; only two rank packages surfaced.
- PvPHQ HQ (base) rank full perk list not indexed; exact price of the 11,500-coin pack unclear.
- No evidence found either way of battle passes on any of these servers.
- PvP Land store prices not found.

## Q2: Which features are must-haves vs differentiators for practice servers in 2025-2026 (and who pioneered/is known for each)?

### Takeaway
Must-haves are now: free kit-based duel queues (ranked + unranked) across many kits including modern 1.21 kits (Sword, Axe, Mace, Crystal/Netherite OP, UHC, Pot, NethPot, SMP), a kit/layout editor, parties (team duels, FFA, party split), FFA arenas, per-kit Elo leaderboards, web profiles/ladders, tournaments/events, and a strong anti-cheat. Differentiators are integrated tier testing (MCTiers-style HT1-LT5) and ranked with tier ratings (PvPHQ), configurable AI bots (PvP Land), seasonal ranked ladders with prizes (Minemen), cosmetic progression paths (Hypixel Duels), and client mods/tier tagger integrations.

### Cited Findings

**Core queue/kit features**
- PvP Club: 1v1 ranked and unranked matchmaking across 8+ combat kits; Duels, FFA, Teams (2v2, 4v4 etc.); Kit Editor that lets players reorganize inventory/hotbar layout. — [mclist.io eu.mcpvp.club](https://mclist.io/server/60213); [BoardMC MCPVP guide](https://www.boardmc.com/post/mcpvp-guide-kits-tiers-rankings)
- A fan-made "MCPVP Ranked" mod adds per-kit Elo for Sword, Axe, Crystal, Netherite OP, UHC, Pot and Mace, with 10 placement games per kit (no Elo swings during placement); public beta; supports mcpvp.club, mcpvp.com, minemen.club. — [Modrinth · MCPvP Ranked](https://modrinth.com/project/xwIsZd7D); [9minecraft](https://www.9minecraft.net/mcpvp-ranked-mod/); [YouTube beta re-release](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1gqTRwztNs)
- PvPHQ: 11 gamemodes — Sword, Axe, Mace, Spear Mace, UHC, Pot, NethPot, SMP, DiaSMP, Vanilla, Minecart (per tier tagger addon). — [Modrinth · PvPHQ-Tiertagger-Addon](https://modrinth.com/project/PzNBY0IN); [CurseForge · PVPHQ TierTagger](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pvphq-tiertagger)
- PvP Land: ranked duels, practice bots, custom kits, automated tournaments, cross-version support; modes listed by third parties include NoDebuff, BuildUHC, Boxing, Bridge, Sumo, Gapple, PvP bots, detailed leaderboards, tournaments and replays. — [pvp.land](https://pvp.land/); [VoxelRank · PvP Land](https://voxelrank.com/server/204-pvp-land)

**Ranked, tiers and tier testing**
- MCTiers system: a tier test is a fight vs a gamemode's tester who places you HT1 (High Tier 1) to LT5 (Low Tier 5); players use /tiertest, pick gamemode and region, then are moved to a private arena with the official kit when a regional tester is free; testers judge more than round wins. — [MCTiers guide](https://mctiers.gg/guides/how-to-get-tier-tested); [MCTiers](https://mctiers.gg/)
- MCTiers points: HT1 60, LT1 45, HT2 30, LT2 20, HT3 10, LT3 6, HT4 4, LT4 3, HT5 2, LT5 1; points based on peak tier. — [mctier.us tiers explained](https://mctier.us/tr/guides/minecraft-pvp-tiers-explained); [MCTiers](https://mctiers.gg/)
- MCTiers server address conflicts across sources (playmctiers.com, mctiers.network, mctiers.ca, mctiers.com). — same sources plus [PvP Club Tier Test](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- PvP Club hosts its own tier testing page (mcpvp.club/tiertest), referencing tiers "on mctiers.com and on PvP Club for that gamemode" (i.e., tiers recognized/linked to MCTiers). — [PvP Club - Tier Test](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- PvPHQ uses a tier ladder HT1-HT5 / MT1-MT5 / LT1-LT5 (per third-party addon) with "TR" points on ladders (e.g., UHC #1 at 276 TR, 15,685 players ranked; another tracker showed 23,586); a "Ranked Update" released Tier 2 and Tier 1, overhauled low-tier ranked, improved matchmaking, recalibrated ranks. — [Modrinth addon](https://modrinth.com/project/PzNBY0IN); [UHC Ladder · PVPHQ](https://pvphq.com/leaderboard/uhc); [flowpvp.gg UHC](https://flowpvp.gg/leaderboard/uhc); [The Ranked Update - PvPHQ (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXbmOuCsPfI); [Tier 1 vs Tier 1 (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mAHeb_MSntY)
- Other servers now market "training tiers" (McTrainer.Club "From One Run to HT1"). — [mctrainer.club](https://mctrainer.club/announcements/from-one-run-to-ht1-how-training-tiers-work)
- Minemen: ranked ladder with numbered seasons (Nodebuff/Debuff at Season 14), "Top ELO" leaderboard, tournaments with real prizes; rules ban Elo/stat boosting and alting in ranked (alts allowed only if both stay out of top 20). — [minemen.shop](https://minemen.shop/); [Minemen rules](http://minemen.club/rules)

**Bots**
- PvP Land is known for configurable AI bot opponents with adjustable difficulty (2023 review: easy to impossible); listicles describe it as "all about practice when fighting a bot." — [pvp.land](https://pvp.land/); [mcserverpost 2023 (older)](https://mcserverpost.com/2023/09/10/pvp-land/); [Sportskeeda 2025](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/10-best-minecraft-pvp-servers-2025)

**Cosmetic progression**
- Hypixel Duels "Duelist's Odyssey": divisions by wins, 11 divisions per mode with 5 levels each (except Ascended), plus an all-modes path; rewards include titles, prefix icons (e.g., at 100,000 wins), kill effects, victory dances. — [Hypixel Wiki](https://hypixel.fandom.com/wiki/Duels/Duelist%27s_Odyssey)
- Social/clan features: Minemen sells "club" creation (up to 50 members on PRO) and friends lists. — [Minemen ranks](https://store.minemen.club/category/ranks)

**Anti-cheat**
- Minemen is known for its in-house anti-cheat ("AntiGamingChair"); site advertises "Anti-Cheat v3.2 ... 40% reduced false positive rate"; listicles say players come for its anti-cheat. — [Minemen fandom](https://minecraftservers.fandom.com/wiki/Minemen_Club); [minemen.shop](https://minemen.shop/); [Sportskeeda 2022](https://sportskeeda.com/minecraft/5-best-minecraft-pvp-servers-practice-2022)
- Plugin landscape 2025-2026: Grim = most recommended free (prediction-based movement engine, low false flags, needs per-gamemode config); Polar = paid, generally rated strongest detection with low server load, aimed at established PvP networks, some report lag sensitivity; Vulcan = cheaper paid, easy setup, mixed reports on PvP servers, often paired with Grim. Sources are blogs/forums, some vendor-written. — [mcvoting 2026](https://mcvoting.com/blog/best-minecraft-server-anti-cheat-plugins); [Sheriffguard 2026](https://www.sheriffguard.com/blog/minecraft-anticheat-comparison); [SpigotMC "Best AC 2025?"](https://www.spigotmc.org/threads/best-ac-2025.687332/); [ArkFlame](https://fairplay.arkflame.com/articles/top-minecraft-anticheats-by-server-type)

**Low latency / regions**
- Minemen is described as low-latency with precise hit detection; MCTiers queues are region-based. PvP Club runs a separate eu.mcpvp.club node. — [Sportskeeda 2025](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/10-best-minecraft-pvp-servers-2025); [MCTiers guide](https://mctiers.gg/guides/how-to-get-tier-tested); [mclist.io](https://mclist.io/server/60213)

**Web & ecosystem**
- PvPHQ has web leaderboards per mode, match history, and third-party client "Tier Tagger" mods showing tiers above player nametags; PvPHQ runs per-gamemode Discord servers. — [pvphq.com ladder](https://pvphq.com/leaderboard/overall); [CurseForge TierTagger](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pvphq-tiertagger); [pvphq.com/discord](https://pvphq.com/discord)

### Inferences
Must-haves (table stakes, 2025-2026):
- Free kits, instant ranked/unranked queues, many 1.21 kits (Sword, Axe, Mace, Crystal/NethOP, UHC, Pot, NethPot, SMP/DiaSMP, Vanilla) plus legacy 1.8 modes (NoDebuff, Sumo, Boxing, BuildUHC) for older-combat players.
- Kit/layout editor saved per kit; parties (team duels, FFA, split), FFA arenas, spectating.
- Per-kit Elo, leaderboards, web profiles/ladders; anti-boosting rules.
- Strong anti-cheat (Grim free baseline; Polar for big networks; in-house at Minemen).
- Region-aware servers/matchmaking (EU/NA nodes), Discord community.
Differentiators:
- Integrated tier testing and recognition of MCTiers tiers (PvP Club), or a fully automated tier/TR ranked system replacing manual tests (PvPHQ's ranked update, appears to be the 2026 trend).
- Client-mod ecosystem (tier tagger nametags, ranked Elo overlay mods).
- Seasons with prizes (Minemen), automated tournaments (PvP Land), host-able events as paid perks (Minemen, PvPHQ, PvP Club "Host Battle").
- AI bots with difficulty levels (PvP Land), replays (PvP Land listed).
- Progression cosmetics earned through wins (Hypixel Duels) and vanilla cosmetic customizers (PvP Club).
- Clubs/clans (Minemen).

### Gaps
- Could not verify which server "pioneered" each feature with primary sources; attributions above are "known for", not "first to".
- No source found on alt detection, appeal systems, Discord rank sync, death messages, or ping-based matchmaking implementations for these specific servers.
- Hypixel Duels' current (2026) feature set not verified beyond the fandom wiki.

## Q3: What do players complain about or praise regarding these servers?

### Takeaway
Very little community sentiment was retrievable: no Reddit threads surfaced for PvP Club or PvPHQ. Available praise is from listing sites/listicles (polished practice, fast matchmaking, balanced kits for PvPHQ; anti-cheat and low latency for Minemen; bots for PvP Land). Minemen's Trustpilot is mixed, with anti-cheat complaints.

### Cited Findings
- PvPHQ praised (by listing site, not players) for polished Practice PvP, fast matchmaking, balanced kits, competitive player base. — [BoardMC · PVPHQ](https://www.boardmc.com/server/pvphq)
- Minemen Trustpilot: one review says the anticheat is "nearly nonexistent"; another credits the anti-cheat as covering for spammer/scammer issues (mixed). — [Trustpilot · Minemen Club](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/minemen.club)
- Minemen praised in listicles for low latency, precise hit detection and anti-cheat (promotional tone). — [Sportskeeda 2025](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/10-best-minecraft-pvp-servers-2025); [Sportskeeda 17 best 2025](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/best-minecraft-pvp-servers)
- PvP Land known/praised for bot fights. — [Sportskeeda practicing list](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/5-best-minecraft-servers-practicing)
- Third-party tier/Elo numbers conflict between official and tracker sites (pvphq.com vs flowpvp.gg), a transparency issue players may notice. — [pvphq.com UHC](https://pvphq.com/leaderboard/uhc); [flowpvp.gg UHC](https://flowpvp.gg/leaderboard/uhc)
- PvPHQ store coins page contradicts itself on /gift and /shop use with coins (potential source of buyer confusion). — [PvPHQ · Coins](https://store.pvphq.com/category/coins); [1000 Coins](https://store.pvphq.com/package/7568481)

### Inferences
- Recurring practice-server pain points in the available evidence are anti-cheat effectiveness, Elo/stat boosting with alts (Minemen bans it explicitly), and store clarity; recurring praise points are fast queues, low latency and good kits.
- Copycat/lookalike store domains (mcpvp-club.com) are a risk worth warning players about.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/MinecraftPvP, r/admincraft) threads were returned by search for any of these servers; YouTube review content beyond PvPHQ's own ranked-update videos was not found.
- No reviews of mcpvp.club from players were found.
