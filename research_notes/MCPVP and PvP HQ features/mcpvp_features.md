# MCPVP / PvP Club (mcpvp.club) and MCPVP.com: feature research

Research method note: WebFetch could not reach mcpvp.club, modrinth.com or builtbybit.com from this session (DNS blocked by the session network policy). Every finding below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited pages, not full page reads. Treat details as "snippet-level" and re-verify on the live pages if precision matters. Research date: 2026-11-19.

IMPORTANT DISAMBIGUATION: there are two linked but distinct things called "MCPVP":
1. **PvP Club** at **mcpvp.club** (eu.mcpvp.club for EU). A modern-combat practice server with duels, parties and FFA, and a Tebex store at store.mcpvp.club. Its tier tests feed MCTiers.
2. **MCPVP.com**. Dream and DrDonut bought MCTiers and relaunched it as MCPVP.com, announced 2026-04-28. It is a ranked platform (website plus server at IP mcpvp.com) with kit tiers, points, profiles, match history and 38 regions. The mcpvp.club EU listing now points players to mcpvp.com/tiers for leaderboards, and a mod lists mcpvp.club and mcpvp.com side by side ([mclist.io](https://mclist.io/server/60213); [9minecraft](https://www.9minecraft.net/mcpvp-ranked-mod/)). The exact corporate or technical relationship (same network, partner, or merged) is **not confirmed** by any source found.
Also beware: **mcpvp.com before 2026** was a different, much older network. **mcpvp-club.com** is a separate "store" site whose ranks and prices don't match the official store, so it is likely unofficial or a copycat. **mcpvp.live** ("PVP CLUB" tier test and leaderboard) is a third-party site, not on the official domain.

## Q1. Game modes, kits, versions and combat

### Takeaway
PvP Club is a 1.21.x modern-combat practice server (join on 1.21.2+, 1.21.11 recommended). It offers ranked and unranked 1v1 duel queues across about 8 kits, FFA arenas, and party modes: team duels 2v2/4v4, party FFA, Host Battle, and custom party fights. MCPVP.com adds a larger kit catalog split into Skill, Vanilla and Community kits, including Spear, Shield, Creeper, Cart, Diamond SMP, Netherite Pot, Bow and Crystal, plus a "legacy" tier view. No source confirms 1.8 combat on either server.

- **Spear** kits and the 1.21.11 recommendation mean both servers run **1.21.11 modern (1.9+) combat** with the new spear and mace items. No source mentions 1.8/legacy-combat queues on PvP Club, so a 1.8 mode would set a competitor apart.
- No source mentions **bot** fights on either official server. The "PvP Club" Bedrock addon with bots is an unrelated fan pack ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/pvp-club)).

### Gaps

## Q2. Ranked system (Elo, tiers, tests, seasons, leaderboards, decay)

### Takeaway
PvP Club uses **staff-run tier tests** with LT5..HT1 per gamemode, a 30-day retest cooldown, separate per-gamemode Discords, and current and peak tiers that are shown on MCTiers and PvP Club. MCPVP.com moves to an **automated** model: matches feed a Player Rating, which unlocks a Tier Test, while a falling rating triggers a "Tier Defense" match. Tiers carry point values (HT1 400+), and Season Zero/beta tiers will be reset.

### Cited Findings
**PvP Club**
- Tier test: a tester duels you and places you somewhere from **LT5 up to HT1** for that gamemode — [mcpvp.club/tiertest](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- Retest cooldown: **once every 30 days per gamemode** — [mcpvp.club/tiertest](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- You request tests in **#request-test** in *each gamemode's own Discord*, not the main PvP Club Discord — [mcpvp.club/tiertest](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- A **premium Java account** is required to test — [mcpvp.club/tiertest](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- **Current tier and peak tier** are tracked. The current tier is always the most recent test result, and it is "what is shown on mctiers and PvP Club" — [mcpvp.club/tiertest](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest)
- The server advertises an "ELO-Based Rankings" / tier system with global leaderboards tracked at mcpvp.com/tiers — [mclist.io](https://mclist.io/server/60213)
- MCTiers context: players are tested in 8 gamemodes, placed in Tier 1–5 (HT/LT), and ranked on one overall leaderboard — [mctier.site](https://mctier.site/) (a mirror or fan site, not the official one)
- Third-party bots and tools "auto-join MCPVP/MCTiers queues" for tier tests. This shows tier-test waitlist queues are high-demand and contested — [queuesniper.com](https://queuesniper.com/); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mKGj2TyD7b4)

**MCPVP.com**
- Flow: regular matchmaking feeds a **Player Rating**, which can qualify you for a **Tier Test**. If your rating falls below what your tier expects, you may be required to play a **Tier Defense Match** against someone from that tier — [BoardMC guide](https://www.boardmc.com/post/mcpvp-guide-kits-tiers-rankings); [EloTiers summary](https://elotiers.com/guides/how-minecraft-pvp-ratings-work)
- Tiers are per kit (HT3, MT3, LT3 shown on profiles). Note the **MT (mid tier)** label, which is new versus classic HT/LT — [BoardMC](https://www.boardmc.com/post/mcpvp-guide-kits-tiers-rankings); [mcpvp.com/tiers](https://www.mcpvp.com/tiers)
- Points thresholds: **HT1 = 400+**, HT2 = 250+, LT2 = 150+, and so on. Example rows: HT2 with 285 points, LT2 with 156 points, "High Tier 3 (143 Points) NA" — [mcpvp.com/tiers](https://www.mcpvp.com/tiers); [/tiers/shield](https://www.mcpvp.com/tiers/shield); [legacy](https://www.mcpvp.com/tiers?version=legacy)
- The highest *starting* tier is reported as LT5. That source is uncertain — [search summary citing BoardMC/mcpvp.com](https://www.boardmc.com/post/mcpvp-guide-kits-tiers-rankings)
- "These Tiers are **BETA TIERS** and will be reset at the end of beta" — [mcpvp.com/tiers/mace](https://www.mcpvp.com/tiers/mace)
- **Season Zero** and tier columns — [YouTube video description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bwwp3dg3xRs)
- Overall titles from "Combat Rookie" through "Grandmaster" — [BoardMC server page (snippet)](https://www.boardmc.com/server/mcpvp)
- Leaderboards have a **region column and filters** — [mcpvp.com/tiers](https://www.mcpvp.com/tiers)
- Planned per the press: "seasonal ranked ladders", "dynamic matchmaking based on real-time skill ratings". This is a lower-quality source — [GuruGamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/minecraft-powerhouses-dream-and-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-for-epic-pvp-platform-launch-26713)

**Community ranked layer (unofficial, not server features)**
- The "MCPvP Ranked" client mod adds its own Elo for mcpvp.club, mcpvp.com and minemen.club. It uses ranks Coal → Copper → Iron → Gold → Lapis → Emerald → Diamond → Netherite, with divisions I–III. The first 10 games per kit are placements, and it awards bonus Elo for beating MCTiers-tiered players — [Modrinth mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/mcpvp-ranked); [9minecraft](https://www.9minecraft.net/mcpvp-ranked-mod/); [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mcpvp-ranked)
- "McPvP.com Tier Tagger" mod shows players' MCPVP tiers above their heads — [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/DCdFdNnR)

### Inferences
- The players clearly want a **visible per-kit Elo/division ladder**, since the community built one in a mod. A competitor that shows an official, real-time version on the server (tab list, nametags, holograms) covers a demand the community is now meeting with mods.
- Tier decay on MCPVP.com is in effect handled by **Tier Defense** rather than time decay. That is inferred from the guide, and no time-based decay was found.

### Gaps
- Exact Player Rating formula, K-factor, placement count, test-unlock thresholds, and season length. No public source.
- Whether PvP Club's own in-game ranked queue uses Elo numbers visible to players.

## Q3. Queue, matchmaking, match start, arenas, rematch, spectating

### Takeaway
Very little official detail is public. What can be confirmed: a hotbar item opens a queue GUI, there is region selection and switching (4 regions on PvP Club, 38 on MCPVP.com), queue priority is a paid perk, and profiles record the map for each match. Nothing was found on cages or countdown animations, map voting, rematch or spectating.

### Cited Findings
- In-game **region switching** for lower latency: NA (Ashburn VA), EU (Frankfurt DE), AS (Singapore), AU (Sydney) — [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/server/pvp-club); [anyserver.pro](https://anyserver.pro/server/224/pvp-club)
- Separate EU node at eu.mcpvp.club — [mclist.io](https://mclist.io/server/60213)
- MCPVP.com: **38 server locations** across 7 regions (NA 14, EU 8, LATAM 5, Asia 5, Middle East 2, Africa 1, Oceania 3) — [BoardMC guide](https://www.boardmc.com/post/mcpvp-guide-kits-tiers-rankings); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/news-dream-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-launch-competitive-mcpvp-platform)
- **Queue priority when the server is full** (VIP+) — [store.mcpvp.club VIP+](https://store.mcpvp.club/package/6877860)
- Queue opened via a sword hotbar item, then a GUI — [horizon issue #11175](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/issues/11175)
- Match records include **kit, map, opponent and score**, which implies multiple named maps per kit — [mcpvp.com profile example](https://www.mcpvp.com/@CPVPVOID); [@pzmp](https://www.mcpvp.com/@pzmp)
- "Automated anti-cheat duels" (MCPVP.com) — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/news-dream-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-launch-competitive-mcpvp-platform)

### Inferences
- "Score" in the match history suggests first-to-N **rounds** formats (for example, first-to-3) at least on some kits. This is inferred.

### Gaps
- No public info on match-start cages or elevators, countdown style, map voting, rematch buttons, /spectate, or arena regeneration. These need in-game observation or YouTube footage review, which was not possible here.

## Q5. Cosmetics, parties, clans, tournaments, Discord, website, anti-cheat, moderation

### Takeaway
PvP Club's monetisation centres on **item customisation** rather than particle cosmetics: armor trim, shield pattern, shulker colour and item-name customisers, name colour and display name, plus bigger parties and more kit slots. Tournaments are advertised as 24/7 automated with prize payouts. Discord is split per gamemode for testing.

### Cited Findings
- **VIP (Permanent) – $19.99**: Armor Trim Customizer, Shield Pattern Customizer, Shulker Color Customizer, Customize Item Names, +3 kit slots (6 total), party size 8 → 16, purple name colour plus tab priority — [store.mcpvp.club VIP](https://store.mcpvp.club/package/6877832)
- **VIP+ (1 Month) – $9.99**: requires VIP. Display Name Customizer, queue priority when full, +9 custom kit slots (12 total), party size 32, Host Battle party mode. Expires after 30 days — [store.mcpvp.club VIP+](https://store.mcpvp.club/package/6877860); [Ranks category](https://store.mcpvp.club/category/ranks)
- Default party cap is 8 — [VIP](https://store.mcpvp.club/package/6877832)
- An unofficial-looking "MCPVP Club Store" (mcpvp-club.com) lists different perks: /fly at spawn, death messages, particle trails, killstreak alerts. It **conflicts with the official store, so it is likely not genuine** — [mcpvp-club.com](https://mcpvp-club.com/)
- Party-recruiting culture: players post "222 for axe" in chat, and a community mod turns that into clickable invites — [Party Inviter (Modrinth)](https://modrinth.com/project/ZHWdM4YU); [Party Joiner │ PVPCLUB](https://modrinth.com/project/7hKTtzVz)
- **24/7 automated tournaments**, with "$15,000+ paid out" claimed. This is self-reported — [mclist.io](https://mclist.io/server/60213)
- MCPVP.com: "big events to organize the competitive scene", tournaments in the feature list — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/news-dream-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-launch-competitive-mcpvp-platform); [BoardMC server page](https://www.boardmc.com/server/mcpvp)
- Discord: the main PvP Club Discord plus separate per-gamemode Discords with #request-test — [tiertest](https://mcpvp.club/tiertest). The website has store, rules and tiertest pages — [Discord listing](https://discord.com/servers/mcpvp-club-1477487779498692722)
- The MCPVP Discord reportedly passed 42,000 members after the announcement — [search summary of YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dyWv1kpUyqI)
- Anti-cheat: "automated anti-cheat duels" on MCPVP.com. The name of the anti-cheat was not disclosed — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/news-dream-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-launch-competitive-mcpvp-platform)

### Inferences
- PvP Club sells **no pay-to-win items**. Paid perks are cosmetic customisation plus convenience (queue priority, kit slots, party size). Monetising vanilla-item visuals (trims, shield banners, shulker colours) suits 1.21 well.

### Gaps
- No kill effects, trails, titles or tags catalogue found on the official store. Clans/guilds, report or appeal systems, and staff structure: not found.

## Q6. Notable unique features and 2024–2026 timeline

### Takeaway
The biggest 2026 event is Dream and DrDonut's purchase of MCTiers and the launch of MCPVP.com: alpha around April–May 2026, beta tiers on the site, 38 regions, automated rating and tests. PvP Club (mcpvp.club) is closely tied to it through tier testing and leaderboards.

### Cited Findings
- Announced **2026-04-28** in the McTiers Discord. The team "spent nearly two years building MCPVP.com", with 38 regions, automated anti-cheat duels and big events — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/news-dream-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-launch-competitive-mcpvp-platform); [X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2049089421916655978)
- The reported purchase price was $300k, after allegations against the former MCTiers owner. Unverified — [GuruGamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/minecraft-powerhouses-dream-and-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-for-epic-pvp-platform-launch-26713)
- Alpha-phase gameplay videos exist on IP mcpvp.com — [YouTube "I tried Dream and DrDonutt's new server"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dyWv1kpUyqI); [YouTube "MCPVP BETA"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vHF41C7dNsY); [YouTube "Is This The Future Of Minecraft PvP?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCaZGC_g30E)

- Prediction markets asked whether open beta would happen by 2026-07-31. That is not confirmation — [WahooPredict](https://wahoopredict.com/en/events/will-dream-and-drdonuts-mcpvp-mctiers-successor-open-beta-by-july-31-2026)
- The press claims it is open source. Unverified — [GuruGamer](https://gurugamer.com/pc-console/minecraft-powerhouses-dream-and-drdonut-acquire-mctiers-for-epic-pvp-platform-launch-26713)

### Inferences (competitor takeaways)
- What sets them apart: (1) tiers recognised across the community (MCTiers, now MCPVP.com) with web profiles, (2) a very large region footprint, (3) a large kit catalogue including the new 1.21.11 Spear, Shield, Creeper and Cart kits, (4) automated rating → test → defense flow, (5) cosmetic-only monetisation through vanilla item customisers.
- Weak spots a competitor could exploit: tier-test queues are bottlenecked (queue-sniper bots exist), there is no official in-game Elo/division display (filled by mods), no confirmed bot practice, no confirmed 1.8 mode, and beta resets.

### Gaps
- Changelogs and patch notes (Discord announcements) could not be retrieved. The current status of open beta and whether mcpvp.club and mcpvp.com have merged are both unknown.
