## The 10 rules (from batch 01)
1. Change the visual on the **word onset** (±1 frame), not on the sentence.
2. Prefer **continuous transformation** (one object becomes the next) over cuts. Save hard cuts for beat hits.
3. **1–3 words per beat**, giant (25–100% frame height) or tiny: no mid-size captions.
4. **2–3 fonts max**: a condensed heavy sans + an italic serif + a UI grotesk.
5. **One accent colour** on a neutral or monochrome base; colour only where it means something.
6. **Depth**: text behind the subject, collage over text, DOF, 3D tilt, parallax.
7. **Motion:** ease-out expo on travel, overshoot spring on pops, motion blur on every fast move, a camera that never fully stops.
8. **Pacing contrast**: strobe bursts (2-frame shots) next to 1.5 s holds.
9. **Sound mirrors meaning**: a sub hit on "hit"/"fired", a whoosh on every move, a riser into reveals.
10. **Bookends**: a simple opening card, a rich middle, a clean end card / cut to black on the last word.
