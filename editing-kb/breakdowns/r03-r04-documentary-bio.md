**Pacing:** slow cold open (4.6 s of no voice), then cuts every ~0.6 s during the collage, then held B&W shots under the last line.

## Voice and sound findings (measured)
- **Neither VO has a discrete echo/delay throw.** The envelope autocorrelation shows no repeating peaks and the spectrograms show no repeated word copies after phrase ends. The voices are dry, close and compressed.
- The **"deep/serious feeling" comes from the bed, not from processing the voice**: a constant low drone, a **sub impact on the first reveal** (r03 2.7 s, r04 0.4 s) and a sustained low tone that keeps ringing after the last word (r04 5.8–6.7 s). A dry voice over a deep bed reads as authoritative.
- Loudness is about -16.5 to -17 LUFS integrated.
- The **echo throw** effect (repeats of the last word fading out) is a separate technique to use *on purpose* on a punchline or name. Recipe in `../voice-fx.md`.

## Signature techniques to reuse
1. **Cold open with no VO** (2–4 s of SFX + macro shots) before the name drop.
2. **Iris/circle mask** to reveal the subject.
3. **Type-on name + small bio** (documentary lower-third, but large and top-left).
4. **Glowing money counter behind the subject** with motion blur while rolling.
5. **Giant stencil name behind a halftone B&W portrait.**
6. **Ember particles over B&W footage** as the only warm colour.
7. Monochrome footage + one accent colour (red or green) for all graphics.
