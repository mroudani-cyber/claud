## r02: "POV: you create animations for the brands you use in real life" (9:16, ~18 s)
The motion is shown *inside a monitor* on a desk at night (screen-in-scene framing), with the TikTok caption at the top. Content: a white 3D product world (white ribbons/arrows, orange Amazon-colour accents), kinetic words ("Any", "items", "need", "Delivery", "My Orders") moving along curved 3D arrows, an alarm clock + boxes for delivery, rows of 3D cards in perspective, and the logo resolve "amazon" (letters assemble with a smile arrow). Takeaway: **brand-colour minimalism** (white + one brand colour), 3D ribbons/arrows as transitions, words riding paths.

## Cross-reference: universal moves from batch 01
1. Continuous transformation instead of cuts (r01, r05, r09, r10, r11).
2. One accent colour + a neutral base, used on glow, type and UI.
3. Word-at-a-time or 1–3 words per beat, swapping on the VO/beat.
4. Rebuilt vector UI, tilted in 3D, with DOF and bloom.
5. Overshoot springs on pops (0.8→1.05→1), ease-out expo on travel, motion blur on every fast move.
6. Bookend structure: simple opening card, a rich middle world, a simple logo/URL close.
